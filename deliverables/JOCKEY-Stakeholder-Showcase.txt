SLIDE 01
JOCKEY
Racing information. Connected.
Stakeholder showcase | October 2026

Speaker notes:
JOCKEY brings the racing calendar, programs, participant histories and published results into one membership experience. This showcase covers the implemented product, its access model and the decisions needed for a controlled launch.

SLIDE 02
Follow the racing day in one place

Discover the meeting.
Study the field.
Review the result.

Speaker notes:
The core experience follows a racing fan's practical questions: What is scheduled? Who is running? What does the recorded form show? What happened afterward? Calendar, program, horse and participant pages connect through permanent records, so users can move between these questions without starting a new search.

SLIDE 04

Declarations, riders, connections and weights
Recent form and comparable performances

Speaker notes:

SLIDE 06
Membership offers two ways to access racing

DAILY ACCESS                         MONTHLY MEMBERSHIP
PHP 50                              PHP 499
24 hours                            One calendar month

Monthly saves PHP 1,001 compared with 30 daily passes.

Speaker notes:
Both plans unlock the same protected racing information. Daily access lasts exactly 24 hours. Monthly access ends at the same Philippine local time in the next calendar month, with shorter months clamped to their last day. Prices are editable in admin. Renewals extend access after the existing period; there is no automatic renewal. The savings comparison is 30 x PHP 50 minus PHP 499, not a revenue forecast.

SLIDE 11
Launch depends on data, payment and deployment

Authorize the racing data source and publication process.
Connect payments with verified confirmation.
Validate the hosted environment and customer experience.

Speaker notes:
An authorized feed or managed import is needed for real schedules, declarations, results and dividends. The payment integration must validate provider confirmation, amount, currency and transaction reference before granting access. Deployment work includes the target database, HTTPS, secrets, backups and restoration, plus browser/device QA. Operator-specific event definitions require authoritative rules. The information membership workflow is separate from the existing betting and settlement implementation.

SETUP.md: deployment configuration and provider integration requirements.

SLIDE 12
Align on the controlled pilot

Confirm pricing and the intended audience.
Assign data, payment and deployment owners.
Agree on pilot scope and success measures.

Speaker notes:
The immediate stakeholder decision is whether to proceed with a controlled pilot and who owns its external dependencies. Proposed measures include subscription conversion, checkout completion, repeat usage and timely publication of verified racing records. No targets, launch date or commercial outcomes are claimed here. Use the live platform to review the customer journey before committing to a broader release.

PRESENTER WALKTHROUGH
1. Open the homepage and select a populated racing date.
2. Open a meeting while signed out to show the membership requirement.
3. Choose Daily Access at PHP 50 and create an account or sign in.
4. Complete the no-charge checkout and return to the intended racing page.
5. Open a race, inspect its declarations, then open a horse profile.
6. Review participant connections, recorded form and published results.
7. Save a favorite and open My membership to show the access period.
